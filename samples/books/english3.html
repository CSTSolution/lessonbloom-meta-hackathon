English 3 – LessonBloom sample book

Written by the LessonBloom team for testing. Not an official textbook.

Unit 5: Animals

New words: cat, dog, bird, fish, rabbit.

Pattern: What is it? It is a cat. It is a dog.

Dialogue: Nam: What is it? Mai: It is a rabbit. I like rabbits.

Unit 6: Colours

New words: red, blue, green, yellow, pink, orange.

Pattern: What colour is it? It is red. It is blue.

Dialogue: Nam: What colour is it? Mai: It is yellow. My favourite colour is yellow.

Practice: point to a pencil and ask "What colour is it?". Pupils answer "It is green."

Unit 7: My family

New words: mother, father, sister, brother, grandma, grandpa.

Pattern: Who is she? She is my sister. Who is he? He is my father.
